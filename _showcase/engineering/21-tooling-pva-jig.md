        <div class="build-log">
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Why</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">起因</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Every joint on a morphing skeleton is an assembly failure point. To delete them I wanted the bearings and threaded inserts <strong>co-cured directly into the structure</strong> &mdash; which means the tooling must hold them in exact position while fibre is wound straight over them.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">变形骨架上每一个关节都是一处装配失效点。要把它们消掉，就得让轴承与螺纹嵌件<strong>直接共固化进结构内部</strong>——这意味着工装必须在纤维直接缠绕其上时，把它们精确地固定在正确位置。</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Snag</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">卡点</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Then the tooling is <strong>trapped</strong>. Once the joint region is wound over, there is no draw direction and no split line that gets a conventional jig back out. A jig you cannot remove is not a jig.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">于是工装被<strong>困住了</strong>：关节区一旦被纤维包覆，就不存在任何脱模方向或分模面能让常规工装退出来。拆不出来的工装不叫工装。</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Call</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">取舍</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Split the tooling by <em>what it has to survive</em>. The trapped core is printed in <strong>water-soluble PVA</strong> and simply thrown away chemically; the precision-critical parts (bearing blocks, Y-shaped chord holders, threaded tow catchers) are <strong>reusable translucent PETG</strong> &mdash; translucent on purpose, so fibre placement can be inspected <em>during</em> winding rather than judged afterwards.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">按"<em>各部分必须活下来吗</em>"来拆分工装：被困住的芯体用<strong>水溶性 PVA</strong> 打印，最后以化学方式"扔掉"；对精度关键的部分（轴承块、Y 形弦杆夹持件、带螺纹的捕束器）则做成<strong>可重复使用的半透明 PETG</strong>——特意用半透明，好让纤维铺放能在缠绕<em>过程中</em>被检查，而不是事后才判断。</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Proof</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">验证</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">24&nbsp;h ambient stand, 60&nbsp;&deg;C&nbsp;/&nbsp;6&nbsp;h cure, then the core dissolves out in <strong>35&nbsp;&deg;C water in 3&ndash;4&nbsp;h</strong>. Bronze bushings come out already co-cured in place &mdash; final assembly is reduced to driving <strong>M2 screws</strong> through each bearing into the tow catcher as the pivot axle.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">常温静置 24&nbsp;h，60&nbsp;&deg;C&nbsp;/&nbsp;6&nbsp;h 固化，随后芯体在 <strong>35&nbsp;&deg;C 水中 3&ndash;4&nbsp;h</strong> 溶尽。青铜衬套出来时已经共固化到位——最终装配只剩把 <strong>M2 螺钉</strong>穿过每个轴承拧入捕束器，充当转动轴。</span></div>
