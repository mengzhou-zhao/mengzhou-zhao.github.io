                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">A T-joint wound with the original hand-held resin bath, once tow size dropped to <strong>3k</strong>. The defect is not hidden inside &mdash; it is <strong>written across the surface</strong>.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">纤维束规格降到 <strong>3k</strong> 之后，用原有手持树脂槽缠出的 T 形节点。缺陷并不藏在内部——它<strong>直接写在零件表面上</strong>。</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Read</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">判读</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">The tempting reading is "I need more practice". The honest reading is that the process had <strong>no mechanism constraining resin pick-up at all</strong> &mdash; it worked on coarse tow by luck of scale, and stopped working the moment the tow got finer.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">最诱人的判读是"我再多练练就行了"。诚实的判读是：这道工序<strong>压根没有任何机构去约束树脂携带量</strong>——它在粗纤维束上能用，只是沾了尺度的光；纤维束一变细，它立刻就不成立了。</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="build-log-row">
                <div class="build-log-key"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Lesson</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">教训</span></div>
                <div class="build-log-val"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">A process that depends on operator feel does not scale &mdash; and it makes every downstream measurement unfalsifiable, because a bad number can always be blamed on the hand. <strong>Uncontrolled variables must be designed out, not practised away.</strong></span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">依赖操作者手感的工艺不可能放大——而且它会让下游的每一个测量都无法证伪，因为任何一个坏数据都能推给"手没稳"。<strong>失控的变量要靠设计消掉，而不是靠练习绕过。</strong></span></div>
            </div>
