    <h5 class="mb-3"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">How I work</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">我的工作方法</span></h5>
    <div class="row">
        <div class="col-md-3 col-6 mb-3">
            <div class="method-step">
                <div class="method-step-num">01</div>
                <div class="method-step-title"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Build what's missing</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">缺什么就自己造</span></div>
                <div class="method-step-body"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">No rig measures what I need? Then the rig is part of the job. Every experiment on this site ran on equipment I designed and built myself.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">没有设备能测我要的量，那台设备就是工作的一部分。本站的每一项试验，都跑在我自己设计并搭建的台架上。</span></div>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-3 col-6 mb-3">
            <div class="method-step">
                <div class="method-step-num">02</div>
                <div class="method-step-title"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Measure the part, not the rig</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">测结构，不测台架</span></div>
                <div class="method-step-body"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Before trusting a reading I prove the fixture isn't in it &mdash; an inclinometer at double load, or a separate unloaded reference frame that subtracts the rig's own compliance.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">在相信一个读数之前，先证明台架没混进去——用倾角仪在两倍载荷下校核，或用独立不承载的参考框架把台架自身柔度扣掉。</span></div>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-3 col-6 mb-3">
            <div class="method-step">
                <div class="method-step-num">03</div>
                <div class="method-step-title"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Chase the root cause</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">追根因，不调参数</span></div>
                <div class="method-step-body"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">When model and measurement disagree I don't tune coefficients until they match. I go back to the hardware, find the defect, and put the defect in the model.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">当模型与实测对不上时，我不会调系数把曲线凑齐。我回到实物上找出缺陷，再把缺陷本身建进模型。</span></div>
            </div>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-3 col-6 mb-3">
            <div class="method-step">
                <div class="method-step-num">04</div>
                <div class="method-step-title"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">Close with a number</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">用一个数字收口</span></div>
                <div class="method-step-body"><span class="lang-en" lang="en">A change counts only when it is quantified: +105% fibre volume fraction, 4.5% model error, 2.3% rig compliance, R&sup2;&nbsp;=&nbsp;0.997 on deposited mass.</span><span class="lang-zh" lang="zh">一次改动只有被量化才算数：纤维体积分数 +105%、模型误差 4.5%、台架柔度占比 2.3%、沉积质量 R&sup2;&nbsp;=&nbsp;0.997。</span></div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
