abstract: >-
  This thesis investigates the development and optimisation of modular filament-wound composite joints for
  hierarchical space frames, focusing on their application in Wrapped Tow Reinforced (WrapToR) truss beam
  structures. It runs the full chain from manufacturing process to validated design tool: a three-dimensional
  winding process that builds a whole truss joint as a single continuous composite part; finite element models
  validated against dedicated experiments, agreeing to within 4.5%; a graph-theory-based algorithm that plans
  three-dimensional winding paths under manufacturing constraints; and a genetic optimisation framework that
  minimises joint mass and deformation while keeping the result manufacturable. Testing confirmed the
  feasibility of the optimised T-joint designs, with manufacturing imperfections shown to govern the observed
  stiffness. Together the work establishes a route towards scalable, structurally efficient composite truss
  systems for aerospace, automotive and architectural structures.
