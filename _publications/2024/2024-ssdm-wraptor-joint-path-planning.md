abstract: >-
  This paper presents a methodology for generating optimal lattice joint designs using a three-dimensional
  extension of the WrapToR winding method. The central difficulty is producing winding paths for intricate
  joints involving varying numbers of trusses and connection angles. The work addresses this with a path
  planning algorithm underpinned by graph theory and manufacturing constraints, accounting for tow
  continuity, geometric limitations and related manufacturing issues.
abstract_zh: >-

  数量与连接角度各不相同时，如何为这类复杂节点生成可行的缠绕路径。为此，本文给出了一种以图论与制造约束为基础的路径规划
  算法，统筹考虑纤维束连续性、几何限制及相关制造问题。
