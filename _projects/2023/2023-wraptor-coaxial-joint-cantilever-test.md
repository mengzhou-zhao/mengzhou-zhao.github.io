    <p class="mb-1"><strong>Problem</strong> &mdash; Before WrapToR wound trusses can go into real lightweight airframes and space structures, an engineer has to predict the stiffness of the jointed structure at the drawing-board stage &mdash; otherwise no one will trust it with load.     <p class="mb-1"><strong>My role</strong> &mdash; I designed and built the rig and its instrumentation. I designed the validation scheme to eliminate error sources one by one &mdash; verifying fixture rigidity with an inclinometer, averaging repeated readings at six stations along the span &mdash; so that what was measured was the structure itself and not the rig; then used that dataset to test the matrix-structural-analysis (MSA) model.</p>
    <p class="mb-0"><strong>Impact</strong> &mdash; Measured deflections matched the model to within 4.5% &mdash; establishing the matrix-structural-analysis model as a validated design tool, so these lightweight trusses can be designed with confidence and no repeated prototyping, cutting downstream development time and cost.</p>

  An experimental cantilever bending test of a full jointed WrapToR truss structure — a filament-wound coaxial joint connecting two carbon-fibre truss beams — designed to measure the structure's stiffness and validate a matrix structural analysis (MSA) model. I designed the test rig: the structure was cantilevered from a rigid 6061-T6 aluminium bracket, with bonded-in stainless threaded-rod inserts for mounting, and the fixture's rigidity was verified by a 0.05&deg; inclinometer that held a zero reading under an 80&nbsp;N check load (nearly double the test load). Tip load was applied in steps up to 44&nbsp;N with hanging calibrated masses, and vertical deflection was measured at six nodes along the span using a high-precision LVDT, each point averaged over three readings to suppress noise. The measured deflected shapes matched the MSA prediction to within 4.5% at maximum load (4.3% average), confirming the model as a reliable tool for later design-space exploration.

  对一个完整的 WrapToR 桁架连接结构——由纤维缠绕同轴节点连接两根碳纤维桁架梁——进行的悬臂弯曲试验，用以测量结构刚度并验证矩阵结构分析（MSA）模型。试验台架由我设计：结构从一个刚性 6061-T6 铝制支架上悬臂伸出，通过粘接的不锈钢螺杆嵌件进行安装；夹具的刚性由一台 0.05&deg; 精度的倾角仪验证——在 80&nbsp;N 的校核载荷（近乎试验载荷的两倍）下其读数保持为零。端部载荷通过悬挂标定砝码分级施加至 44&nbsp;N，并使用高精度 LVDT 在沿跨度分布的六个节点处测量竖向挠度，每点取三次读数的平均值以抑制噪声。实测挠曲形状在最大载荷下与 MSA 预测的偏差在 4.5% 以内（平均 4.3%），证实该模型是后续设计空间探索的可靠工具。
