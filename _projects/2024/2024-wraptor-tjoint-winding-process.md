summary: >-
  Adapting the WrapToR coaxial-joint winding process to hand-wind three-truss T-joints — branching connections needed for hierarchical space frames. The original hand-held resin bath caused a "tow-catching" defect once tow size dropped to 3k (200 tex), so it was replaced with a guided tow-wetting system: a tow wheel, a precision 0.5&nbsp;mm PTFE tube, and a ceramic eyelet. Thermogravimetric analysis of the wound samples showed the redesign raised the fibre volume fraction from 22.53% to 46.34% (a 105.6% increase) while giving more uniform fibre alignment and fewer resin-rich voids.
